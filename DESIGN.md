# Design notes

How the Mojo Maestro plugin models the circuit, and what was checked.

## The circuit (per channel)

A passive, unpowered diode clipper (Ethan Winer, audioXpress, October 2017).

- R1, 1 kOhm: series resistor from the input; also feeds the output directly
- D1, 1N270 germanium: always in circuit, clips negative peaks
- D2 and its switch, 1N270: Odd adds D2 so both peaks clip; Even is D1 only
- R2, 1 kOhm (Glue) and R3, 120 Ohm (Mojo): the limiting resistors, R3 clips harder
- R4, 10 kOhm linear pot (Amount): a rheostat in series with R2 or R3 to ground, more resistance means less effect
- C1, 0.047 uF: only in Mojo; with R1 it gives a gentle rolloff near 3.4 kHz
- SW1, centre-off: Glue, true bypass, Mojo

Behaviours reproduced:

- The output level falls as distortion rises. There is no makeup gain.
- Amount at its minimum is still not clean. Only Bypass is.
- Clipping starts at an absolute voltage (the diode turn-on), so the model needs a reference level: the Input Reference parameter says how many volts 0 dBFS stands for.
- Stereo: shared controls, independent signal paths per channel.

## The solver

With no load current, node A is one series loop: input, R1, diode(s), limiting resistor plus pot, ground. For each sample:

    Vin = I(Vd) * Req + Vd,   Req = R1 + Rfixed + Rpot + Rs
    Vout = Vin - I(Vd) * R1

- Even: `I = -Is (exp(-Vd / nVt) - 1)`
- Odd: `I = Is (exp(Vd / nVt) - 1) - Is m (exp(-Vd / nVt) - 1)`, where m is the D2/D1 matching ratio (1.0 here)

The root is found with a safeguarded Newton-Raphson (bisection takes over whenever a Newton step leaves the bracket or stalls), warm-started from the previous sample, so it converges whatever the size of the jump.

Two circuit models are offered:

- Simple: the memoryless clipper, then a fixed R1*C1 low-pass in Mojo.
- Coupled: C1, R1 and the diodes solved together at node A (trapezoidal capacitor plus Newton), so the rolloff corner moves with the clipping state, as in the real circuit.

Coupled against a 128x backward-Euler reference (`dsp_tests/test_coupled.cpp`, Mojo, input 0.9 FS, error in dB relative to the reference RMS, lower is closer):

| Case | Simple | Coupled at 1x | Coupled at 4x |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 kHz, Even | -24.7 | -57.5 | -75.1 |
| 5 kHz, Even | -9.4 | -30.2 | -51.9 |
| 10 kHz, Odd | -2.7 | -18.2 | -41.9 |

The Simple model's error does not shrink with oversampling (it is a modelling error). The Coupled model converges.

## The plugin around the solver

- Oversampling (off, 2x, 4x, 8x), linear phase, around the clipper. The analog circuit has no aliasing, a digital one would.
- Reported latency is fixed at the largest setting (65 samples at 8x). Every other setting is padded with a delay to the same latency, so all settings are time-aligned and can be crossfaded.
- Changing the oversampling setting runs the old and the new path side by side for 20 ms (4 ms warm-up, then a 16 ms smoothstep crossfade).
- Changing Mode, Harmonics or Circuit Model keeps the old configuration running and crossfades to the new one over 10 ms.
- Changes never overlap. A change that arrives during a running fade (or together with another kind of change) waits until the fade is over and is then crossfaded on its own.
- Amount, Input Reference and Output Trim are smoothed over 20 ms.
- The DC blocker (5 Hz high-pass; Even mode creates a DC offset) always runs, and its switch crossfades over 10 ms.

## The display

The display is drawn by the editor, over a plain smoked-glass window in the faceplate picture. It has 13 character cells of 16 segments each, plus a separate heart. Segment polygons (already sheared and separated by a small gap) and the letter table are generated by `art/pipeline/vfd_geometry.py` into `Source/VfdGeometry.h`, so the picture pipeline and the editor share one geometry.

Each frame is built at 8 work pixels per design pixel: unlit segments as a faint ghost, a honeycomb mesh that dims what lies behind its wires, the lit segments with three Gaussian blooms of different widths, three fine filament wires, a soft-knee tone curve so bright parts roll off instead of clipping, then an area-average down to the window with a bezel-weighted blend. The result is cached and redrawn only when the shown characters, the heart or the fade level change.

Text behaviour: the idle loop alternates two 7 second phases with 120 ms fades. The first phase holds "GLORY TO I AM" for 2.4 s, steps one character every 200 ms to "I AM WHO I AM" and lights the heart from 4.4 s. The second shows "CODE BY SFOG7". A read-out shows the control name left-aligned and its value right-aligned in the 13 cells; a `.` in a text lights the previous cell's decimal point.

## Checks

- `dsp_tests/test_datasheet.cpp`: the diode respects the 1N270 limits, Bypass is exact, silence stays silent, DC output matches an independent solution of the loop, Even clips the negative side only and Odd clips both.
- `dsp_tests/test_coupled.cpp`: the table above, plus a stress test (Nyquist bursts, noise, 8 V drive stays finite and stable).
- `Tests/click_test.cpp`: runs the whole processor on a sine and measures the largest second difference around every setting change (single, simultaneous and overlapping) and at the start of playback. A change passes when its kink is less than 1.5 times the steady-state kink.
- `Tests/interaction_test.cpp`: drives the editor with real mouse events and checks the mapping of every control to its parameter.
- `Tests/snapshot_test.cpp`: paints the editor in each display state (idle messages, scrolling step, all eight read-outs) and checks that the text is lit and that the heart is lit only at the end of the first message.
- Steinberg's VST3 validator and pluginval (strictness 10).

## Known approximations

- The diode model (Is 5 uA, n 1.75, Rs 2 Ohm) is chosen to fit the 1N270 datasheet limits. It has not been measured on a real part.
- The source is ideal and the load is open circuit (Winer's own test used 10 kOhm).
- Fixed temperature (Vt 25.9 mV); no component tolerances.
